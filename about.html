About Our School

Welcome to Our School

Sharda English School, Diksal is an ISO 9001:2015 certified English medium school managed by Vidya Prasarak Mandal. Established in 2002, the school provides quality education from Nursery to 10th Standard following the Maharashtra State Board (SSC) curriculum.

Set in a calm and student-friendly environment, the school is committed to academic excellence, discipline, and strong moral values. Through experienced teachers and a structured curriculum, we create a supportive atmosphere where every child is encouraged to learn and grow.

Address: Diksal, Post Koshane, Taluka Karjat, District Raigad – 410201, Maharashtra.
