About Our School

Established in 2002, Sharda English School, Diksal is a Maharashtra State Board (SSC) English medium school offering education from Nursery to 10th Standard. The school is committed to providing quality education in a disciplined, caring, and student-friendly environment.

We believe that every child has the potential to learn and grow when guided with care, values, and the right academic support. With dedicated teachers and a strong focus on both academics and character building, Sharda English School strives to nurture confident, responsible, and capable individuals prepared for the future.
